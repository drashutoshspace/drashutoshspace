# Ashutosh Mishra

**Postdoctoral Researcher** · Dynamic Legged Systems lab, Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT), Genova

<a href="https://github.com/drashutoshspace"><img src="https://komarev.com/ghpvc/?username=drashutoshspace&style=flat&color=12468f&label=Profile+views" alt="Profile views" /></a>

I build legged and modular robots that can work on the lunar surface, and the learning-based control that lets them keep working when the terrain does not cooperate.

**Now**<br>
Learning-based locomotion controllers for quadrupedal robots in space exploration, with Claudio Semini under the ALTEC project. Most of my time goes into GPU-accelerated reinforcement learning in Isaac Lab, and the sim-to-real pipelines that decide whether a policy survives contact with real hardware.

**Before**<br>
Doctorate at the Space Robotics Lab, Tohoku University, with Kazuya Yoshida, as a Japanese Government MEXT scholar. I worked on MoonBot, a modular, reconfigurable robot for lunar base construction under JST Moonshot Goal 3, field-tested at the JAXA Space Exploration Test Field in Sagamihara and in the Abukuma Cave.

## Research

- [**Legged locomotion for space**](https://drmishra.space/research/legged-locomotion-for-space). How does a legged robot keep walking on terrain that no one has surveyed, with no chance of rescue?
- [**Modular reconfigurable lunar robots**](https://drmishra.space/research/modular-reconfigurable-lunar-robots). If you can only land a fixed mass on the Moon, should it be one specialised machine or a set of parts?
- [**Learning and autonomy for field robots**](https://drmishra.space/research/learning-and-autonomy-for-field-robots). What has to be true for a learned policy to still work outside the simulator?
